## 0.1.0

- First release: `DashView` and `DashStage` for single birds (seven poses,
  four facings, seven accessories, expressions, hover and tap reactions).
- `DashFlockView` draws whole flocks in one canvas, with `DashCamera`,
  `DashCameraController` and `DashCameraGestures` for pan and zoom.
- `DashMeadowView` and `DashPlanetView` scenes, `DashFlockFlight` for flights
  between routes.
- `DashPalette` and `DashTerrain` theme extensions, light and dark.
